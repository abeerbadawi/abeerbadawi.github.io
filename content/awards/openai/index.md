Awarded the **OpenAI AI and Mental Health Research Grant** as Principal Investigator for the project:

**Preventing Cognitive Atrophy in LLM Mental Health Support Through Psychometric Evaluation and Therapist-Aligned Design**

The project investigates how large language models used for mental-health support can be evaluated and designed to reduce behaviours associated with cognitive atrophy, over-reliance, and reduced user autonomy.

**Funding:** CAD $26,000 in research funding, with additional API access and ChatGPT Pro support.
